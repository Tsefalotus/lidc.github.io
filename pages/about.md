# LIDC - Life in Different Colors

Videographer & Video Editor focused on producing cinematic visual content for brands, creators, and digital platforms.

I oversee the full production process — from shooting and editing to color grading and final delivery — shaping each project with careful attention to detail, pacing, and visual consistency.

Every piece of content is created to do more than look good: it should tell a clear story, strengthen identity, and hold the viewer’s attention from the first frame to the last.

By combining creative direction with a structured workflow, I deliver videos that feel intentional, polished, and aligned with each client’s goals.
